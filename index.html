rclone drive

A personal tool that connects one person's Google Drive to their own computer.

rclone drive is a private OAuth client used with rclone, an open-source command-line program. It lets its owner mount and sync their own Google Drive files on their own Linux laptop.

The app is used by a single person, its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses

Only the Google Drive of the account that the owner signs in with. Access happens directly between the owner's computer and Google. No data passes through any server operated by this app.
