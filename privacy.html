Privacy policy

rclone drive · Last updated: November 2026

rclone drive is a private OAuth client used by a single person, its owner, to access their own Google Drive with rclone. This policy explains how data is handled.

Data we collect

The app does not collect, store, or transmit user data to any server of its own. It has no backend, no database, and no analytics.

How Google data is used

When the owner signs in, Google issues an access token. rclone stores this token in a configuration file on the owner's own computer. The token is used only to read and write files in the owner's Google Drive, at the owner's request. File contents go directly between Google and the owner's computer.

Sharing and disclosure

Google user data is not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training of any model.

Retention and deletion

The token stays on the owner's computer until it is deleted. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
